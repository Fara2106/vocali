Privacy policy

Vocali for iPhone and iPad · updated November 6, 2026 · Italiano

In short: Vocali collects no data. Voice messages, transcripts and summaries stay on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you turn on iCloud, transcripts, summaries and senders are also stored in your private iCloud, which we cannot read.

Who is responsible

Vocali is developed by Lorenzo Faraoni, an individual. For any privacy question write to vocali.support@icloud.com.

What the app does with your voice messages

  • Transcription happens on your device with Apple’s speech recognition framework. Audio is never uploaded to any server.
  • Title, summary and key points are generated on your device by the Apple Intelligence model, when it is turned on.
  • Language detection (the “Automatic” setting) also happens on your device.
  • History: transcripts, summaries and the sender you type are stored in the app’s storage on your device.
  • iCloud (optional, off by default): if you turn on “Save history to iCloud” in Settings, titles, transcripts, summaries and senders are stored in the private database of your iCloud account, so you find them on your devices. The service is run by Apple under its privacy policy and we cannot read this data. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Audio: the app keeps a temporary copy of the file only until the transcription is finished, then deletes it.

For each language (Italian, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese) iOS downloads the transcription model from Apple the first time it is needed, or when you download it from the app’s Settings. The download is handled by the operating system under Apple’s privacy policy and contains none of your voice messages. The language you choose in Settings is stored only in the app’s preferences on your device.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No usage analytics, ads or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Notifications

Result notifications are local: the app creates them on your device, without any server. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications → Vocali.

Reports (“Tell us what’s wrong”)

If you choose to send a report, the app prepares an email in your mail app. Besides the text you write, it contains only the device model and the iOS and Vocali versions, which you see before sending. The email is sent only if you send it. We receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply and fix the problem. If you want one of your emails deleted, write to us.

How to delete your data

To delete a voice message, touch and hold it and choose Delete, or swipe it left in the history. If you delete the app, iOS removes all its data from your device. If you turned on iCloud, turn it off in Settings and delete the copy from your device’s iCloud settings.

Children

Vocali collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with the new date.
