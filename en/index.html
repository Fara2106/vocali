Support

Found a problem or have a suggestion? In the app open the ? menu and choose Tell us what’s wrong, or write to vocali.support@icloud.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I transcribe a voice message I received in a chat?
In Vocali tap Add Shortcut and confirm in Shortcuts. Then, in the chat, touch and hold the voice message, choose Forward, tap the Share icon and then Trascrivi con Vocali. You get a notification when the transcript is ready.

I can’t see “Trascrivi con Vocali” in the share sheet.
Scroll to the bottom and tap Edit Actions to add it. If you deleted it from Shortcuts, add it again with the Add Shortcut button in Vocali.

Which languages are supported?
Italian, English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, for both transcription and summary. In Vocali → Settings (the gear) choose the language of your voice messages, or “Automatic”: the app recognizes the language of each message among the downloaded ones.

I only get the transcript, not the summary.
Summaries are made from 20 seconds up (you choose the length in Settings) and need Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). On devices that don’t support it, voice messages are only transcribed.

The first transcription is slow.
The first time you use a language, iOS downloads its transcription model: you need a connection only once, then it works offline. You can download models in advance from Vocali → Settings.

I got “Long voice message paused”.
iOS suspended the app before the end. Open Vocali and the transcription resumes where it stopped.

Do my voice messages end up on a server?
No: transcription and summaries happen on your device. If you turn on iCloud, the text is also stored in your private iCloud; audio is never uploaded. Details are in the privacy policy.
